# AML-04 Customer due diligence and expected activity

Chakshu Demo Bank (fictional). Owner: BSA Officer.

1. At onboarding the bank records each customer's business type, industry, home country
   and expected monthly activity.
2. Activity above 3 times the expected monthly amount needs an explanation. Without one,
   the analyst escalates (AML-01) and recommends an enhanced due diligence (EDD) review.
3. EDD review: confirm the business is real and active, and request the source of funds
   through the relationship manager as a normal business request, without mentioning
   suspicion.
4. Activity that does not fit the stated industry (for example, a clinic receiving many
   large transfers from unrelated businesses in one day) is a red flag even below 3 times.
5. High risk-rating customers get the same review at 2 times expected activity.
