# REF-01 Regulatory references (public US guidance, summarised)

Summaries for the demo. Read the original documents for the exact wording.

- **SAR rule for banks, 31 CFR 1020.320.** Report suspicious transactions of USD 5,000 or
  more; file within 30 days of initial detection, or up to 60 days if no suspect is
  identified; keep records 5 years; SARs are confidential.
  https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/subtitle-B/chapter-X/part-1020/subpart-C/section-1020.320
- **FFIEC BSA/AML Examination Manual.** How US examiners assess suspicious activity
  monitoring, alert review and SAR decisions.
  https://bsaaml.ffiec.gov/manual
- **FinCEN Advisory FIN-2022-A002, Elder Financial Exploitation (June 2022).** Red flags
  such as sudden large withdrawals or transfers by an older customer, new people added to an
  account, and transfers to new payees.
  https://www.fincen.gov/sites/default/files/advisory/2022-06-15/FinCEN%20Advisory%20Elder%20Financial%20Exploitation%20FINAL%20508.pdf
- **FinCEN Alert on "pig butchering" virtual currency investment scams (September 2023).**
  Red flags such as customers sending growing amounts to virtual currency platforms after
  meeting someone online.
- **FinCEN SAR advisory key terms.** The key term to put in a SAR when a FinCEN advisory
  applies.
  https://www.fincen.gov/resources/suspicious-activity-report-sar-advisory-key-terms
