# AML-03 Account takeover and fraud referral

Chakshu Demo Bank (fictional). Owner: Head of Fraud, with the BSA Officer.

1. Treat these as account-takeover signals when they appear within 72 hours before the
   suspicious transfers: a password reset, a login from a new device, a login from outside
   the customer's home country.
2. When takeover signals are present, refer the case to the fraud team the same day. The
   customer may be a victim, not a launderer.
3. The fraud team, not AML, contacts the customer, and only about securing the account.
   It never mentions the AML investigation (see AML-02, no tipping off).
4. A login from abroad on the customer's usual device, with no password reset, is often
   travel. Note it, but it is not a takeover signal on its own.
5. The SAR decision still follows AML-02: a victim's account used to move illicit funds can
   still require a SAR.
